# Third-party data

- `hipparcos_mag65.json`: Hipparcos catalogue (ESA 1997, I/239), stars brighter than V = 6.5.
- `constellation_lines.json`: constellation figures from [d3-celestial](https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial)
  (`data/constellations.lines.json`), Copyright (c) 2015, Olaf Frohn, BSD 3-Clause licence. Converted to a
  compact list of J2000 polylines; used only to draw the figures of the calibration panel.
