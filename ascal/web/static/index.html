ascal Automatic astrometric calibration of all-sky cameras
Code Lumaria

Calibrate your all-sky camera from one night image

Give the site and the UTC time of a clear night frame. ascal finds the stars, identifies them without any prior calibration, mask or manual step, and fits the mapping between pixels and the sky (altitude, azimuth): optical centre, focal length, fisheye distortion and tilt of the camera.

Fisheye cameras that see most of the sky, with stars down to magnitude 4–5. JPEG, PNG, TIFF, FITS or camera raw. The image and the results are deleted from the server after 6 h.

The mid-exposure instant is what matters: an error of 10 s moves the sky by 0.04°.

ascal is the calibration software of the Lumaria
    light-pollution monitoring network (Allande Stars). Method: J. R. González Fernández, L. Hermosa Muñoz, M. Fernández Alonso,
    L. González Cuesta, Automatic astrometric calibration of low-cost all-sky cameras (2026, submitted).
    Star positions from Hipparcos (ESA 1997); constellation figures from d3-celestial (O. Frohn).

  
Uploaded images are used only to compute the calibration and are deleted after the job; results expire after a few hours.
